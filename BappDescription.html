CSRF Review Assistant

An evidence-driven, Community-compatible CSRF and CSWSH review workflow for passive discovery, explainable prioritization, safe Repeater variants, reliable offline PoCs, and privacy-aware reporting.

The extension correlates browser-ambient authentication, login/OAuth workflows, token signals, scoped SameSite cookies, Origin, Fetch Metadata, CORS, content types and methods, GraphQL operations, and WebSocket handshakes. It never sends test requests automatically and never presents passive evidence as a confirmed vulnerability. Guarded active verification is disabled by default and requires explicit in-scope selection and confirmation.
