CSRF Review Assistant

An evidence-driven, Community-compatible CSRF review workflow for passive discovery, explainable prioritization, safe Repeater variants, offline PoC generation, and privacy-aware reporting.

The extension correlates browser-ambient authentication, sensitive actions, token signals, SameSite, Origin/Referer evidence, content types, state-changing GET requests, and GraphQL mutations. It never sends test requests automatically and never presents passive evidence as a confirmed vulnerability.
